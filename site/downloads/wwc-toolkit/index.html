How to use the worksheets

  1. Unzip the folder and copy it into your book's project folder. A production subfolder keeps everything in one place.
  2. Give 00_Production_Run_Sheet to Claude and say: "Run my production."
  3. Claude fills in what it can. You do the account actions — the tasks marked Author. Claude should never ask for your passwords.
  4. Write the briefs before you hire anyone. Have Claude draft them from your Canon and voice profile, then rewrite the parts that are you talking.
