Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Marque Publishing ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain, the same way we try to write everything.

What we collect

  • Email address — only when you choose to sign up for our newsletter or a free download. Signup uses double opt-in, so you confirm before we ever send you anything.
  • Purchase information — if you buy a book through our store, our payment processor collects the details needed to fulfill your order. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic, anonymous usage data — aggregate page views and traffic sources, with no cookies and no personal profiles.

How we use it

To send you the emails, tools, and updates you asked for; to fulfill book orders; and to understand, in aggregate, which pages are useful so we can improve the site. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone. Ever.

The services we rely on

  • MailerLite — email signups and newsletters.
  • Shopify / Lulu — book sales and fulfillment.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and privacy-first, cookieless analytics.
  • Google Fonts — typography.

Each of these has its own privacy practices for the data it handles on our behalf.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our analytics are cookieless. If you submit a signup form, your browser may store a small amount of data to remember that — nothing more.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom of every message. You can also ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we hold about you — just email us and we'll take care of it.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top. Material changes will be noted clearly.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@marquepublishing.com and a real person will answer.
