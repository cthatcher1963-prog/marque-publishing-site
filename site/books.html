Books

Fiction and nonfiction across genres. Each one started as an idea that wouldn't let go.

Chris Thatcher

Author · Speaker · Advisor

I spent thirty years in cybersecurity — advising executives, building programs, and trying to make complicated things simple. Somewhere along the way I realized I had more to say than I could put into a whitepaper or blog. Writing was always part of how I expressed myself, but lately it's become something bigger. It's a way to share what I've learned and what I've been carrying around inside for years.

Last year, a good friend and mentor gave me a nudge and told me I needed to write my book. Here I am today with multiple books in the works. They aren't just about security or business. They are the stories and ideas that wouldn't leave me alone. If something I have to say speaks to you, I hope you'll reach out and share your own story.

Writing With Claude

Your voice, amplified — not replaced.

Marque AI Collection · Writing · Nonfiction

The sequel to Creating with Claude goes deep on one thing: the written word. It answers the fear every writer has about AI — "won't it just make me sound like everyone else?" — and flips it. The whole book is about teaching Claude to sound like you, and using it to write more, better, and faster without losing the thing that makes it worth reading.

Part memoir, part workshop, it walks the real loop: draft, react, revise, revise again, until it's your voice on the page and not the machine's. By the end, you're not fearing the tool. You're conducting it.
