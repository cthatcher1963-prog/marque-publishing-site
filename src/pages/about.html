The team

Meet The People Behind The Work

Chris Thatcher

President & Publisher

Chris spent thirty years in cybersecurity — advising executives, building programs, and trying to make complicated things simple. Somewhere along the way he realized he had more to say than he could put into a whitepaper or blog. Writing was always part of how he expressed himself, but lately it's become something bigger — a way to share what he's learned and what he's been carrying around inside for years.

Last year, a good friend and mentor gave him a nudge and told him he needed to write his book. Today he has multiple books in the works. They aren't just about security or business. They are the stories and ideas that wouldn't leave him alone. If something he has to say speaks to you, he hopes you'll reach out and share your own story.

Jayden Thatcher

Head of Game Development & Engineering

Jayden leads the software and game development side of Marque Publishing. He brings the engineering mindset and the gamer's instinct — the rare combination of knowing how to build something right and knowing when it's fun.

Currently building the tools and games that will define Marque's software division. More details coming soon.
