A few words about me

I spent roughly thirty years in cybersecurity — advising executives, building programs, and trying to make complicated things plain. Somewhere along the way I realized I had more to say than a whitepaper could hold.

So I started writing. Not just about security, but about the stories and ideas that wouldn't leave me alone: a thriller set in the Canary Islands, a poker book for people who play for fun, and whatever shows up next. The common thread is simple — if it keeps me up at night, there's probably a book in it.

This site is the start. Nothing's published yet, and I'm fine with that. If you want a note when the first book is ready, the signup below is the place.
