# Marque Publishing — Press, Social & Marketing Plan

Companion to `LAUNCH-PLAN.md` (which owns the dates and deadlines). This document owns the *messaging, channels, and assets*. Prepared July 5, 2026. Launch: Saturday, July 18.

## Positioning — one sentence each

**Creating with Claude** — "A 30-year cybersecurity veteran shows regular people how to build real things with AI — no jargon, no hype, a working project at the end of every chapter."
Audience: curious professionals, side-project builders, non-technical makers. Tone: warm, practical, generous.

**Cyber Risk is Business Risk** — "The book every board member should be handed with their orientation packet — three decades of the field, distilled to the decisions that are actually theirs to make."
Audience: board members, CEOs/CFOs, security leaders buying for their bosses. Tone: authoritative, plain-spoken, zero FUD.

**The studio story (cross-cutting):** one author, two books, two genres, same craftsman standard — and the AI book was *built using the method it teaches*. That's the hook no other launch has.

## Press strategy

**Assets (all at /press by Jul 15):** two press releases · bios (50/150/300 words) · headshots · hi-res covers · fact sheet per book · interview Q&A.

**Distribution — organic and direct, no paid wire.** A $300 PRWeb blast gets indexed and ignored; a personal note gets read.
- **Cyber Risk targets:** cybersecurity trade press (Dark Reading, SC Media, CSO Online, Help Net Security), board-governance outlets (NACD community, Directors & Boards), your LinkedIn network — which IS the target market.
- **CwC targets:** AI-newsletter authors, productivity podcasts, local business press (founder-launches-publisher angle), maker communities.
- **Podcast pitch list:** 8–10 shows across both audiences; pitch email drafted from the Q&A doc. Podcasts convert better than articles for books — prioritize these.
- Personal pitch notes go out launch week from Chris; releases live on /press for anyone who bites.
