# Marque Publishing — Brand Spec for Cover Artist

## Colophon Mark: The Lozenge (2A)

The publisher's mark is a diamond (lozenge) shape containing a serif "M" letterform. It has three elements:

1. **Outer diamond** — Ink stroke
2. **Inner diamond** — Brass stroke (smaller, concentric)
3. **Top accent diamond** — small solid Brass diamond centered above the top vertex
4. **M letterform** — centered, Cormorant Bold (or Canela if available)

The mark appears on the **spine** (small, bottom) and optionally on the **back cover** (in the lockup with the wordmark).

## Color Palette

| Name     | Hex       | RGB              | CMYK (approx)       | Use                          |
|----------|-----------|------------------|----------------------|------------------------------|
| Ink      | `#15212E` | 21, 33, 46       | C:85 M:65 Y:45 K:75 | Primary dark, backgrounds    |
| Brass    | `#B68A4E` | 182, 138, 78     | C:15 M:30 Y:65 K:20 | Accent, borders, highlights  |
| Oxblood  | `#7C2A33` | 124, 42, 51      | C:25 M:80 Y:65 K:30 | Accent (dagger, details)     |
| Paper    | `#FBFAF7` | 251, 250, 247    | C:0  M:0  Y:2  K:1  | Light backgrounds            |

**Note:** CMYK values are approximate soft-proof conversions. For exact color matching on press, reference Pantone equivalents:

- Ink → closest to Pantone 533 C
- Brass → closest to Pantone 7557 C
- Oxblood → closest to Pantone 7622 C

## Typography

| Role                 | Font               | Weight   | Fallback              |
|----------------------|--------------------|----------|-----------------------|
| Display / headings   | Canela              | Regular  | Cormorant Bold        |
| Subheadings          | Tiempos Headline    | —        | EB Garamond Medium    |
| Body / UI            | Inter               | Regular  | Helvetica Neue        |
| Mark letterform (M)  | Cormorant           | Bold 700 | Times New Roman Bold  |

The wordmark "MARQUE" uses Cormorant Bold with wide letter-spacing (~12 units).
"PUBLISHING" beneath uses Inter Regular with letter-spacing (~8 units), set in Brass.

## Spine Layout

- Background: Ink (#15212E)
- Text: "MARQUE PUBLISHING" in Inter Medium, letterspaced, Paper (#FBFAF7), rotated 90° counterclockwise (reads bottom to top)
- Small Lozenge mark at the bottom of the spine

---

## What to Place on the Cover

### Back cover
- Stacked lockup (mark + wordmark) — typically bottom center or bottom right
- Use the color version on light backgrounds, reversed on dark

### Spine
- "MARQUE PUBLISHING" text + small Lozenge mark at bottom
- Always reversed (Paper/Brass on Ink)

### Front cover
- The publisher's mark does NOT typically appear on the front cover — that's the book's own design space
