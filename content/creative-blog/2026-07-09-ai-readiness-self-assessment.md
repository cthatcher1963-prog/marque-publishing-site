"Where do I even start?" is the question I hear most from smart, busy people about AI. Not "is it useful" — everyone suspects it is. They just don't know their starting point.

The AI Readiness Self-Assessment answers that in about ten minutes. It's not a quiz that tells you you're a "Curious Explorer" and sells you a course. It's a working inventory of how you handle the things AI actually changes: how you research, how you write, how you plan, how you verify what you're told.

## What you'll learn about yourself

The assessment scores you across the skills that matter for building with AI — from framing a clear request to checking output you can't personally verify. For each area, your score points you at what to do next: which habits to build first, which chapters of *Creating with Claude* to skip because you're past them, and which ones to slow down on.

Most people discover two things. First, they're further along than they thought — if you can delegate work to a person clearly, most of the skill transfers. Second, their weak spot is almost never prompting. It's verification: knowing when to trust the output. That's learnable, and knowing it's your gap is most of the fix.
