Meet the publisher

Chris Thatcher

30 years in cybersecurityWriterAdvisorAI coach

I spent thirty years in cybersecurity — advising executives, building programs, and trying to make complicated things simple. Somewhere along the way I realized I had more to say than I could put into a whitepaper or blog. Writing was always part of how I expressed myself, but lately it's become something bigger. It's a way to share what I've learned and what I've been carrying around inside for years.

Last year, a good friend and mentor gave me a nudge and told me I needed to write my book. Here I am today with multiple books in the works. They aren't just about security or business. They are the stories and ideas that wouldn't leave me alone. If something I have to say speaks to you, I hope you'll reach out and share your own story.
